Product owner: Wei (GitHub `WFC5114`). Wei approves scope, designs and releases. Plan: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/3f324c09-f9d2-4a6a-8f52-ff7a6d91a831

## Hard facts
- Native Swift + SwiftUI, iOS 26+. Wei's phone: iPhone 18 Pro Max, iOS 27.
- Wei has **no Mac**. Nothing can be built locally; GitHub Actions (macOS runner) is the only compiler. A PR is not done until CI is green.
- Xcode project is generated by XcodeGen from `project.yml`. Never commit `*.xcodeproj`.
- Targets: `GymApp` (app), `GymWidgets` (widgets + Live Activities), `GymAppTests`. Code shared by app and widgets lives in `Shared/`.
- App and widgets share data only through the App Group `group.com.wfc5114.gymapp` (`AppGroup`, `SnapshotStore`).
- Data stays on device. No network calls except food databases (Open Food Facts, USDA) and, only if Wei adds a key, the Claude API.

## Design rules (binding: read `docs/DESIGN_LANGUAGE.md` before any UI work)
- The app is ONE machine, the Laboratory Measuring Deck: a triangular prism on #000000 with DIET, TRAIN and BODY faces. No tab bars, menus or pushed screens.
- Feel benchmark: the Not Boring apps, learned only from public material (never decompiled). Juice on every commit, lit 3D numerals, layered recorded + synthesised sound.
- Swipe edge to edge to rotate the prism 120° (8% overshoot). Orthographic camera.
- Working contract: `docs/ART_DIRECTION.md` (Wei's Revision 2 prompt; Wei's decisions at its end override it). How it was applied, adopted proposals and the PASS/FAIL/NOT TESTED checklist: `docs/DESIGN_REVIEW.md`.
- DIET: real food logging (Wei). A tall glass MEASURING JAR (Wei, 13 Oct; replaced the deck and slabs). LOG FOOD opens an in-face intake inset (search, barcode, serving) that forms a block; drag it into the jar (it swells to its jar size) or tap LOAD. Blocks are chunky, sized by kcal, tumble in (spring 1.0/220/15), settle at angles on a deterministic pile, overflow past the etched TARGET, and the jar bobs (3.5/450/22). Tap a block to edit it. 50/100/250/500 kcal are quick-add with unknown macros. Contact commits exactly once; edit, delete and undo.
- TRAIN: a DESK HOURGLASS (Wei, 13 Oct, from reference photos; elegant and minimal), in clay since 14 Oct: indigo and cream plates, turned orange posts in butter sleeves, round smooth thick glass, pale sand, knurled rest knob. Adjustable rest; glass size follows rest length. SET DONE logs the set and flips the glass (1.0/800/35) with a brief shake. Wall-clock time is the truth; every rest and every added block of time is logged for the coach. Coach recommends rest per body part, adapted daily.
- BODY: a jointed clay ARTIST'S MANNEQUIN (Wei, 14 Oct; replaced the figurine) on a dial turntable, labelled schematic: InBody/Health fat fills the waist, belly, hips and limbs, lean broadens shoulders and limbs; the scale trend moves fat between scans and a ±5% training tilt follows logged sets (both labelled estimates); dashed outline of 8 weeks earlier. Breathes, shifts weight, glances; tap it and it flexes. Scrubbed by a vernier ruler (numbers 1.2/180/28); the turntable swings with the drag and springs back.
- BLENDER IS THE SOURCE (Wei, 14 Oct): rendered faces (DIET and TRAIN) are pictures rendered from `design/render/night_diet.py` / `night_train.py` (shared studio in `night_kit.py`) placed by `night-diet.json` / `night-train.json`; code only does behaviour. Change the look in Blender and re-render, never in code. BODY's mannequin stays live 3D.
- CLAYMORPHISM everywhere (Wei, 14 Oct): stylised clay objects that are complex and tactile (hinges, screws, seams, knurls, raised lettering), with idle life; hard objects stay hard. BODY, the hourglass and the jar are done (jar and hourglass: thick glass, Wei 14 Oct).
- Damped springs only (mass/stiffness/damping). No easing curves.
- Every event has a specified sound (AVAudioEngine) and Core Haptics profile (see the sensory table). No presets or system beeps.
- Widgets and the Dynamic Island are typographic: text on black, light-to-heavy "strike" on change; island is a flat gauge.
- Skins (NIGHT default per Wei 14 Oct: graphite clay trays, ivory clay, glass, sans labels, keys and gauges as lit clay/glass objects in the scene (`App/Machine/ClayParts.swift`); FLARE colour, MONO matte black, CONCRETE, SIGNAL) bundle palette, material, physics and sound kit. Defined in `Shared/Brutal.swift`; `-skin <id>` on launch overrides.
- RealityKit + Metal (SceneKit is deprecated); text and controls are SwiftUI layers projected with the same orthographic maths. Honour Reduce Motion, silent switch, sound and haptics toggles, VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, 44 pt targets.
- Fixture data, sample data and untested behaviour are always labelled as such.
- Rest and cardio sessions are stored and feed the AI review.
