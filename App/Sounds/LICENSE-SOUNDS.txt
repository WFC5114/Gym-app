Recorded impact layers in this folder come from Kenney's free sound packs,
licensed CC0 1.0 (public domain): http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

- plate_*, metal_*, wood_*, glass_*: "Impact Sounds" by Kenney (www.kenney.nl)
  https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
  zip: https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/impact-sounds/87b4ddecda-1677589768/kenney_impact-sounds.zip
  (impactPlate_heavy/light, impactMetal_heavy, impactWood_heavy, impactGlass_light, variants 000-004)
- click_*: "Interface Sounds" by Kenney (www.kenney.nl)
  https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
  zip: https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/interface-sounds/fa43c1dd4d-1677589452/kenney_interface-sounds.zip
  (click_001-005)

Processing (scripted, ffmpeg): mono, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit CAF, trailing silence
removed, loudness normalised to -16 LUFS with a -1.5 dBTP ceiling. In the app
each one is layered under the synthesised material sound, with random variant
choice and bounded pitch variation.
